UDISE+ field dictionary

Every column India’s school census releases. What the form asked. What the codes mean. Which years carry it. What changed under a name that stayed the same.

Almost every public number about Indian schooling starts in UDISE+. Its columns are not stable. 79 stopped at the 2022-23 break. 33 changed name. One keeps its name and changes meaning. A yes/no is often a type code with eight values.

This page is for anyone who must know whether a number means what it appears to mean. A researcher building a panel. A journalist checking a claim. A parent reading a figure about their own child’s school. Every note here carries the fix, not only the warning.

Source: Ministry of Education, Government of India — UDISE+ school-level microdata releases 2018-19 to 2025-26, read against the Data Capture Format issued for each year (how this was made).
